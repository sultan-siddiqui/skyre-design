## Brand artwork and fonts

The logo, symbol and crescent pattern SVGs preserve the original artwork supplied in Sky Re's brand kit. The user-supplied `assets/sky-re-crescent-bg.png` is retained unchanged behind the video section.

The footer now uses the user-supplied `assets/skyre-footer-logo.png` across all page views. CSS hides only its transparent outer padding. The Home hero uses the supplied `assets/hero-icon-art.png` as a foreground overlay, with its two primary tips aligned to the right-edge and bottom-edge midpoints of the photograph. Both PNG files remain unchanged; the crescent uses uniform scaling and a small responsive orientation adjustment to fit those points.

Cairo is the approved first-pass font. Four weights are hosted locally. Keep `assets/OFL-Cairo.txt` and `assets/Cairo-font-notices.txt` with the fonts.

## Contact location map

The Contact page embeds a city view of Riyadh from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=12/24.72/46.70). Its contributor attribution remains visible inside the map. A CSS grayscale/sepia filter provides the muted brand treatment. This is a city view, without an unconfirmed office pin; no map screenshot or local copy of the map tiles is included.
